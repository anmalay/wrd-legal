Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the wrd app handles personal data when you use the app and its widgets on iOS and Android. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Anastasiia Iaemurd, Italy. Contact: wrdwidget@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We do not require account registration and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment card details inside the app.

  • Device identifiers: an app instance ID created by Firebase, an anonymous app user ID created by RevenueCat, and, only with your consent, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android).
  • App events: app launches, screens viewed, button taps, selected learning settings, whether a widget is installed, and similar product interaction events.
  • Purchases: trial starts, subscription purchases, renewals and cancellations, and your subscription status.
  • Diagnostics: basic technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate country, collected automatically by the analytics tools.

3. What stays on your device

Your learning preferences, onboarding choices, widget settings, saved words, practice progress, and similar app state are stored on your device using the system storage of iOS or Android. We do not receive them.

4. Why we use your data and on what legal basis

  • Analytics (Google Firebase) — to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Advertising measurement (Meta) — to measure which advertising campaigns lead to installs, trials and subscriptions. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Subscriptions (RevenueCat, Apple, Google) — to provide the premium features you paid for and to check your subscription status. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for purchase records, our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent when you first open it. Until you give it, no advertising events are sent and analytics is limited to cookieless signals without any identifier (Google Consent Mode). If you decline, it stays that way. On iOS, the app also asks for permission before it uses your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency).

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store on iOS and by Google through Google Play on Android. We do not receive your payment card number or other full payment details. RevenueCat receives the anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from the App Store or Google Play.

6. Who receives your data

  • Google (Firebase Analytics; consent management through Google AdMob User Messaging Platform; Google Play).
  • Meta Platforms (advertising measurement).
  • RevenueCat (subscription status).
  • Apple (App Store).

These providers may process data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your personal data. The app shows no ads.

7. How long we keep data

  • Analytics events: 2 months in Google Analytics, after which they are deleted automatically. Aggregated reports without personal data may be kept longer.
  • Advertising measurement data: according to Meta’s retention rules for app events.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to provide your subscription and to meet accounting and tax obligations.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.

To exercise your rights, write to wrdwidget@gmail.com. The app has no accounts, so to help us find your data please include your device type and, for subscription data, the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

9. Your other choices

  • On iOS, you can change tracking permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • If you remove the app, its locally stored data is removed according to your device's behavior and settings.
  • Subscriptions are managed through your App Store or Google Play account.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: wrdwidget@gmail.com.
