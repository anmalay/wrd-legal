Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the wrd app handles information when you use the app and its widgets on iOS and Android.

1. What we collect

We do not require account registration and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment card details inside the app.

We collect limited app usage analytics, such as app launches, screens viewed, button taps, subscription state, selected learning settings, and similar product interaction events. This helps us understand how the app is used and improve stability and product decisions.

To measure our advertising campaigns, the app reports events such as app installs, trial starts, and subscription purchases to Meta. Where you allow it, this can include your device's advertising identifier.

2. What stays on your device

Your learning preferences, onboarding choices, widget settings, saved words, and similar local app state are stored on your device using the system storage of iOS or Android.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store on iOS and by Google through Google Play on Android. We do not receive your full payment card number or other full payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from the App Store or Google Play.

4. Cookies

The app does not use browser cookies in the way a website does.

5. How we use analytics data

  • To understand feature usage and app flow.
  • To improve app quality, usability, and subscription experience.
  • To measure product and advertising performance at an aggregate level.

6. Data sharing

Data may be processed by our service providers: Google Firebase (analytics), Meta (advertising measurement), and RevenueCat (subscription status). We do not sell your personal data.

7. Your choices

  • On iOS, the app asks for permission before it uses your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent when you first open it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • If you remove the app from your device, its locally stored data is removed according to your device's behavior and settings.
  • Subscriptions are managed through your App Store or Google Play account.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: wrdwidget@gmail.com.
